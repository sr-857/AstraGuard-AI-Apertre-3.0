## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security is a top priority for **AstraGuard AI**. We take all security vulnerabilities seriously and appreciate the community's help in discovering and responsibly disclosing them.

If you discover a security vulnerability within AstraGuard AI, please DO NOT open a public issue. Instead, follow these steps:

1. **Contact us directly**: Send an email to the project maintainers or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature if enabled on the repository.
2. **Provide details**: Include as much information as possible. Helpful details include:
   - A description of the vulnerability and its impact.
   - Steps to reproduce the issue.
   - Potential attack scenarios.
   - The version(s) of AstraGuard AI affected.
3. **Wait for our response**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours and strive to provide a preliminary assessment within 5 business days.

### What to Expect

- We will work with you to understand and resolve the issue safely.
- We will notify you when a fix is implemented and give you credit for the discovery in our release notes (if you prefer).
- We ask that you maintain confidentiality until we have had reasonable time to release a fix.

Thank you for helping keep the space security community safe!
